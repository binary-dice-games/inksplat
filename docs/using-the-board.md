# Using the board

The user guide with pictures is the site's [tutorial page](../viewer/src/site/tutorial.html). This is the reference in short.

Keys 1-9 pick the tool (1 pointer, 2 line, 3 text, 4 sticker, 5 eraser, 6 sticky note, 7 pin, 8 thread, 9 colour); pressing the
same number again goes through its options (clicking a tool with options opens a panel with all of them), and a faint line under the canvas buttons says for a few seconds how to use the tool just picked (turned off in **Settings**, or on the join form for viewers). The pointer has four modes: **cursor** (the usual one), **laser** (dragging leaves a glowing trail that fades in a second; a click without dragging makes a ping, rings that spread from the point for everybody) and **emotes** (press, move towards a face on the wheel and let go, or click and then click a face; it floats up and fades, and anybody who clicks it while it lives makes it bigger), and the **magnifier** (the cursor with a round lens beside it that enlarges the screen and the drawings under the pointer, to read small text; the wheel changes how much, and only you see it); Esc goes back to the cursor. When the pointer comes back by itself (after a note is put down, or Esc), the first press of 1 only confirms it. Drag items to move them, click one to
select it (a bar to change it appears over it, hidden while it is dragged) and press Delete or Backspace to remove it. With the pointer, a drag that starts on an empty place selects everything it passes over (as the eraser marks it; both draw a dashed trail along the way); drag any of them to move them all, change their colour from the bar, press Delete to remove them, and Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X and Ctrl+V to copy, cut and paste them (pasted where the pointer is, on the canvas shown; zoomed in, Space+drag or the middle button pans) (anybody can change anybody's, unless the owner locks it from the bar over it: only the owner, or nobody); stickers and links scale with Shift+drag, pictures and files resize from their corner, and all turn with Ctrl+drag (what is pinned turns about its pin). The wheel zooms
at the pointer (0 resets), and image files can be dropped on the board (an animated GIF moves; a picture with a transparent background is cut out, its border and shadow around what shows); web addresses pasted or dropped become cards that open the page, with the site's icon (from DuckDuckGo's icon service, which is sent only the site's name; local addresses keep a globe). A link to a YouTube, Vimeo or Dailymotion video, a Loom, a Google Drive, Docs, Slides, Sheets or Figma file, a Google Maps or OpenStreetMap place (not a `maps.app.goo.gl` short link or directions), or a Spotify track, album, playlist or episode becomes the site's **player** instead (YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode; Google files only if shared or published): click it to use it (it then takes the pointer; a press outside it or Esc gives the board back), drag its top bar or, before clicking, the player itself to move it; lines and notes drawn over it stay on top, and its bar opens the page in a new tab. Every browser on the canvas loads the player from its site; PNG and SVG show a still player. Sticky notes (tool 6) are put down with a click, or dragged out to the size wanted, and written by clicking them, moved by dragging, resized from the grip on its bottom right corner (shown once it is selected) and removed with Delete once selected; select a note to copy its text, change its shape, text size and colour, or lock it. Pressing 6 again goes through the shapes of the note (square, rounded, oval, diamond, hexagon); select your note to change its shape or colour. The pin tool (7) puts down bare pins in five shapes (7 again goes through them); a click on a thread with it splits the thread with a pin there. Select a pin to give it a **label**: the bar has a button for no label (the default) and three for the size of the text (small, medium, large; a size asks for the text, Enter keeps it); drag the label to move it, click it to write it again. **Threads** (8) hang between pins, like on a detective's board: drag between any two points: a pin is put at each end that has none (those of notes, pictures and links, or one from the pin tool, are used when you press on or let go near them), and a click without dragging leaves nothing. Drag from a point of an existing thread to split it in two with a new pin there and pull a new thread from it; the two ends that meet at the new pin have no head, so the thread keeps the heads it had; 8 again goes through the heads (none, source, destination, both); select a thread to change its colour or heads, or Delete to remove it. Dragging a pin moves it, and Shift+dragging from a pin pulls a thread whatever tool you hold (it comes back when you let go), and a pin put on a note, picture, link or sticker stays on it when that moves, turns or scales. A note, picture or link dragged by its pin onto another element is attached to it the same way (it is drawn on top and goes, turns and scales with it). Five **canvases** (round buttons at the top centre, each with the number of people on it; everybody chooses their own and sees only the cursors of those on it) hold separate contents, each on its own background: the presenter picks it for the canvas they are on (**Background of canvas N**: the screen, whiteboard, blackboard, wall or cork; by default canvas 1 has the screen, which shows the stream on screen or black, and the others the whiteboard, blackboard, wall and cork); board files keep all of them, PNG and SVG the one shown, and the clear buttons act on the one shown. **Clear drawings** in the controls removes only lines, texts and stickers; **Clear all** also takes the notes, pins, threads and files. **Ask for a turn** (lower
left) queues you to speak; the presenter can remove anyone. Files (up to 5 MB) dropped on the board
are pinned as previews (the first page of a PDF, the text of an Office file) that everyone can drag
a copy out of (Shift+drag it out; a plain drag moves it); **QR code** in the controls shows the link for a camera.
**Save...** and **Open...** (viewer bar, presenter controls) save the board as a PNG picture, an SVG drawing, or a board
file (`.inksplat.zip`: one JSON file per canvas, the settings, and the pictures and files on the board as they are, so any zip tool shows what is in it) that **Open...** adds to a new session to carry on;
everything opened becomes the opener's own, so it can be moved and changed there.
The presenter page asks before closing or reloading (that ends the session) and keeps a backup of the board in the
browser, written a second after every change; the next session offers to **Resume** it or **Start new**.

## Support sessions

**Start a support session** on the presenter page helps one person with their computer
([support-app.md](support-app.md)). Send them the link (**Copy text and link** has a message
ready). They install inksplat Support once and press **Allow**; their whole screen appears and
sets the shape of the board. The toolbar has only the pointer: the cursor, the laser (a trail,
or a ping with a click) and emotes, which the person sees on their own screen. You see your
marks in the video, a moment late, so they are not drawn twice. Read the **safety code** to the
person: their app shows the same digits; if they differ, end the session. The room takes one
person; if they stop or lose the connection they can share again with the same link.
**Settings > Support** sets the video (15 to 30 frames per second).
