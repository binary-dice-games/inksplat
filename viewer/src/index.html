inksplat

Share a browser tab, a window or your whole screen, and draw on it together. Viewers open a link: nothing to install, no account, no server in between.

Got a link from a presenter? Open it to join the session.

Features

Share anything

A tab, a window or a whole screen, desktop applications included.

Draw together

Lines, text, sticky notes, pins and threads, on five canvases per board.

Peer to peer

Video and drawings go straight between browsers. Nothing is stored on a server.

Save and carry on

Keep the board as a picture, a drawing or a file to open in a later session.

How it works

  1. Start presenting: a shared board that anybody in it can put a tab, a window or a screen on, at any time.
  2. Send the link (or show its QR code) to the people you invite.
  3. Draw together over the live picture: everyone sees everyone's ink.

    Share your screen

    Everything in what you share can be shown to everyone in this session. You choose a tab, a window or a whole screen in the next step, and your browser always shows when you are sharing.

    Save the board

    A board file keeps the notes, threads, pictures and the files pinned on it (up to 25 MB). Use Open… in a new session to carry on: everything in it becomes yours there.

    About inksplat

    inksplat turns a shared tab, window or screen into a shared whiteboard. The presenter shares from this site; viewers open a link, watch a live video of it and draw on it with everyone else. Peers find each other through public trackers and talk directly over WebRTC: there is no inksplat server, and nothing you draw is stored anywhere but in the browsers.
