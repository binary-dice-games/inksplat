inksplat

Present with inksplat

Start a shared board, give people the link, and draw, write and share files together. Nothing to install.

In the session you and everyone else can share a browser tab, a window or a whole screen, even a desktop application, at any time. You choose which one everybody sees.

You draw on a copy of what you share, in this window, so keep this window next to it: anything drawn on the shared screen itself would not reach the others.
